I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), working with Professor Junxian He in the HKUST NLP Group. My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning.

I received my B.Eng. from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024, where I was also advised by Professor Junxian He.

## Research Interests

- Hallucination in vision-language models
- LLM truthfulness and interpretability
